# Master Architecture Cleanup & Smart UI Upgrade Prompt

## Objective
Perform a clean, intelligent, non-destructive architectural refinement of the entire application—eliminating UI clutter, redundant floating buttons, role/mode restrictions, and artificial sci-fi naming ("مجرة"), while upgrading the Settings experience and section cards into a cohesive, refined, alive, and ultra-smart system.

---

## 1. Preserve What Works (Critical Anchor)
- **Keep the Upgraded Top Header Bar:** Preserve the current sleek, compact, smart Top Bar design and adaptive behavior—do NOT bloat or revert it.
- **Keep & Upgrade the 4-Icon Section Preview Feature:** Retain the smart feature where each section card displays **4 clean visual icons/badges** representing what is inside that section (`الخطوط`, `المقاسات`, `الأرقام`, `التقويم`, etc.), and refine its typography, alignment, and micro-motion so labels never awkwardly truncate or feel cluttered.

---

## 2. Remove Redundant Floating "Morrow" Button
- **Completely remove** the floating `Morrow` pill button anchored at the bottom-left of the screen (which currently overlaps cards and content).
- Keep **Morrow** cleanly accessible via its dedicated buttons in the **Top Bar** and **Bottom Navigation Bar**, keeping the viewport unobstructed.

---

## 3. Eliminate Mode/Tier Restrictions & "الصلاحيات ومصفوفة التحكم والخبير" Splitting
- **Remove all mode-based restrictions and artificial capability splits** (such as "وضع الخبير", "وضع المبتدئ", "قراءة فقط", mode-level gating, or restricted access matrices).
- Unify the application into **one single natural default state** where **full capabilities, tools, editing, and controls are natively available** to the user at all times—without needing mode switches or permission-tier toggles.
- Reorganize any useful operational settings (such as table columns, custom categories, or confirmation rules) into clean, natural standard settings without mode/tier framing.

---

## 4. Clean, Natural, Professional Naming (Remove "مجرة" Everywhere)
- **Completely eliminate** the word `"مجرة"` / `"مجرات"` / `"محاور"` sci-fi naming across all headings, cards, breadcrumbs, search placeholders, and buttons (e.g., replace `"مجرة مكتبة الثيمات والملفات"` or `"دخول القسم (3 مجرات)"` with clean, natural, direct names like `"مكتبة الثيمات والمظهر"`, `"الخطوط والترقيم"`, `"الحاسبة المالية"`, etc.).
- Every section and sub-section must use **clear, natural, direct Arabic terminology** that immediately tells the user what it does—no gimmicky labels, no cluttered metadata strings above titles.

---

## 5. Re-Architect Settings into Smart, Alive, Uncluttered Cards & Standalone Pages
- Redesign the **Settings Hub** and all internal settings views to eliminate vertical clutter and visual noise:
  - **Smart Alive Section Cards:** Each settings category appears as a refined, spacious, subtly animated card featuring:
    1. Clear, natural **Section Title** + concise **1-line description**.
    2. The **4 visual sub-item icon tiles** cleanly aligned below (with crisp full or smartly fitted labels, subtle hover/tap spring motion, and alive icon feedback).
    3. Clean full-card tap target (no bulky redundant yellow footer bars repeating the section name).
  - **Independent Full-Page Sub-Views:** Tapping any section card or any of the 4 icon tiles opens its **dedicated, standalone full-page view** connected directly to the live app state, with smooth transitions and clean back navigation.
  - **Refined Visuals & Living Motion:** Use harmonious spacing, calm dark/accent contrast, smooth spring transitions, alive micro-interactions, and full RTL (`dir="rtl"`) polish across every screen.

---

## 6. Strict Non-Destructive Guarantee
- Do **NOT** delete any core features, themes, fonts, calculators, OCR tools, ledgers, agencies, or Morrow AI capabilities—only clean up the naming, remove the obstructing floating button, unify all powers into one default mode, and elevate the visual architecture.
